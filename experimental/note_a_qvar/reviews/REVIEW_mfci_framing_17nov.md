# Review: the MFCI framing as a complete new version (subagent, 17 Nov 2026)

Read-only review commissioned on the author's MFCI idea (17 Nov 2026). Saved in substance; tags as given by the reviewer; spot checks by the main session noted. Not decisions.

## Bottom line
One strong, new claim: weights estimated only from asset co-movement in FOMC windows come out close to weights calibrated to GDP effects (the draft's condition eq:agree, g proportional to S^-1 psi, roughly holds). Worth a JME paper if it leads with the **weights**. Weaker: "similar history" is close to mechanical; "similar GDP impact" is reduced form and cannot distinguish; "levels instead of change measures like FCI-G" is contradicted by the package. The name "MFCI" is taken.

## 1. Claims
- **Weights.** F vs published GS: 10-year 0.609 vs 0.515; equities -0.051 vs -0.056; dollar 0.065 vs 0.069; GS inside all three F 90 percent intervals [0.147, 1.114], [-0.068, -0.030], [0.021, 0.112] [verified, `report/tab_fourw.tex`]. FRB/US 0.590 / -0.032 / 0.103 also inside each interval, but its dollar/equity trade-off is 2.49 times the market's [1.02, 6.8] [verified, FP-A24]. Claim holds for GS; for FRB/US weight by weight, not for the trade-off.
- **Wilks benchmark (new, scratch computation).** Random weights within a factor of 2 of the market weights (log-uniform, 5,000 draws, r*-step groups): level correlation with the market index median 0.988, 5th percentile 0.93; 12-month changes median 0.986. Factor of 4: median 0.964, 5th pct 0.75. Dollar weight times 2.5: 0.956. So the 0.94-0.99 correlations say little about whether weights agree [verified by reviewer; not in package].
- **(a) PC indexes.** "Behave differently" supported; "emphasize spreads" partly: in 12-month changes Baa-10y alone R2 0.18 of NFCI, three assets 0.20 -> 0.51 with the spread; in levels spread alone 0.05, NFCI loads on the 10-year; NFCI puts no weight on the dollar [verified, `10_nfci_on_variables.csv`]. NFCI complements the market flow: significant for unemployment at 12 months (t 3.6), dominates at 24 months and for recessions (t 4.2 vs 1.3) [verified, `10_joint_lp.csv`]. Market-NFCI level correlation 0.52 / 0.55 / 0.61 in different files: reconcile after FP-A62. VAR NFCI rows: appendix only (conversion divides by 0.097, s.e. 0.051; weekly data).
- **(b) GDP impact.** Reduced form; ordering moves numbers more than weights; follows from similar series; fades after a year vs FRB/US; 2-year yield and funds rate weak in the same VAR; FCI-G shock is a flow experiment (own path 0.36 at 4q, -0.31 at 8q).
- **(c) Levels vs changes: contradicted.** For market, GS, FRB/US the 12-month flow carries year-ahead content: unemployment, r* step, flow t 4.9-5.3, level t -0.15 to 0.60 [verified, `09_mean.csv`]; Granger: lag sum (level) p 0.15-0.35, differences p < 0.01 [verified, `09_granger.csv`; main session spot-checked: nominal market p_sum 0.27, p_diff 5e-5]; out of sample levels worse than own lags (RMSE ratio 1.15-1.17). Level adds only without 2008-15 (`12_levelflow.csv`). FP-A14: "flows for rate-based indexes, levels for NFCI/ANFCI". The 12-month change of the market index correlates 0.94 with FCI-G 1-year (FRB/US 0.96); levels 0.30. FCI-G is close to the flow of a similarly weighted index: level vs flow is a transformation choice, separate from weights.
- **(d) Equity "weight".** Separate coefficient (-0.051; FRB/US -0.032; Y -0.027), contribution to a policy move (window 0.40; FRB/US 53-60 percent, stock response -14.5 percent on impact per 1 pp persistent hike), variance share (77-79 percent market and GS levels, 49 FRB/US; 12-month changes 65 and 38). Bauer-Swanson orthogonalized raises the equity weight (-0.070) [verified, `28_information.csv`].
- **(e) Equities in models.** FRB/US already has a stock-wealth channel and its equity response is about twice the window response per policy point [inference]: literally, FRB/US may have too much. Claim holds only for models without the channel; citations needed (CEE 2005; Smets-Wouters 2007; Brayton et al. 2014; Bernanke-Kuttner 2005; Rigobon-Sack 2004; Chodorow-Reich-Nenov-Simsek 2021; Di Maggio-Kermani-Majlesi 2020; Kekre-Lenel 2022; Bauer-Bernanke-Milstein 2023) [secondhand]. One sentence in the conclusion.
- **(f) Name.** MCIs (Bank of Canada, RBNZ, 1990s) [secondhand; Eika et al. 1996 and Batini-Turnbull 2002 in the bib]; Beaton-Lalonde-Luu (2009) call a MUSE-model FCI in interest-rate-equivalent units "MFCI" [verified in `experimental/path_fci/lit/lit_nfci_indexes_weights.md` line 89, main session]; ECB MF-FCI (Bletzinger et al. 2026). Suggest "policy-news FCI" or "announcement-weighted FCI".

## 4. Referee objections
1. Mechanical correlation -> lead with weights and intervals; print the Wilks benchmark.
2. VAR not identified -> reduced form, both orderings, random-weight placebo, FRB/US benchmark, fade as a finding; cite Note B for identified effects.
3. Depends on the news measure (Y halves the equity weight; ratio 2.06) -> justify NS factor; Y and Bauer-Swanson rows in the main text; GS inside F intervals.
4. Information effect / risk premium -> Bauer-Swanson raises the equity weight; risk-premium channel is the point.
5. Level vs flow and PC comparison are straw men -> drop "levels instead of changes"; FCI-G as the flow of a similarly weighted index; PC indexes as complements measuring stress and credit.
