# Colleague review 1 of mp_transmission.tex (1 Nov 2026)
Reviewer: a reviewer agent instructed to act as a supportive senior colleague (no files edited). Full text summarized here; nothing has been applied yet (awaiting the user's choices).

**Overall.** Attractive idea, clean four-step story, right benchmark. Framing not yet modest enough: "resolve much of this", "precise and stable", "neither ingredient works alone", "decisive" are contradicted in part by (i) quadratic trend + FCI also giving large significant unemployment responses, (ii) Kiley r* losing the price result, (iii) with IP in the system the nominal 2-year + FCI already gives significant unemployment at 12 m (0.41/0.36/0.25) and the step-1 puzzle largely disappears (tab3_ip_u, not shown). Robust message: (1) a broad FCI is essential; (2) the slow-moving component of rates must be removed; (3) significant price and IP responses need the LW gap. Most important change: make the propagation assumption the centrepiece, confront it with one new analysis, restate the claim narrowly.

**Main concerns.** A. "Same response whatever moved financial conditions" (equities forecast unemployment; commodity puzzle in MF_DEVELOPMENT 6a): decompose by component; FRB/US- and GS-weighted FCIs; policy-driven FCI component (cumulated surprises or IV); placebo with non-policy news; report the commodity result. B. LW r* filtered from the same macro data, full-sample one-sided vintage: use survey / shifting-endpoint / market r* proxies, real-time LW, pseudo-out-of-sample test; state that the step sequence was fixed in advance by the companion note. C. Weights from the same NS events (0.99 window impact nearly mechanical); weights fixed in bootstrap: re-estimate in each draw or split-sample; reconcile window definitions. D. Inference: daily block not re-estimated; fixed-design monthly bootstrap; 200 draws; no r* uncertainty: recursive-design bootstrap >=1000 draws. E. Monthly block could use 1973+ (and 1973-2020 as in BS); discuss ZLB. F. COVID: 2020 values enter as lags in 2021: dummies or drop through 2021; end-2019 sample. G. Equal within-month weights rejected for core PCE (say so); show the 2-year and FCI paths (HAR may treat the shift as near permanent; Hanson-Stein reversal); PATH in a footnote. H. Shocks: report NS-GB; Jarocinski-Karadi poor man's; Bauer-Swanson orthogonalized.

**Literature.** Drop the "papers we have read" footnote (use "to our knowledge"); credit Gertler-Karadi and Caldara-Herbst as closest work. Add: Faust-Swanson-Wright (2004), Bauer-Rudebusch (2020), Hamilton-Harris-Hatzius-West (2016), Holston-Laubach-Williams (2023), Gilchrist-Zakrajsek (2012), Swanson (2021), Bu-Rogers-Wu (2021), Jacobson-Matthes-Walker, Gazzani-Vicondoa, Francis-Ghysels-Owyang, Hanson-Stein (2015), Plagborg-Moller-Wolf (2021), Brave-Butters.
