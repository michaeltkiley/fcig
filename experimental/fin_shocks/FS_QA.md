Algebra (my formulation, for the user's review): eqs. y = a y(-1) + b f(-1) + u; f = h f(-1) + c i + d n + nu; i = rho i(-1) + phi y + psi f + eps. Removing f gives an equation in y and i with an MA error. Finding, stated in the text: omission alone is not non-invertibility. With psi = d = 0, eps is recoverable and a long VAR gets the right answer. Non-invertibility needs news (d != 0) or policy feedback from f (psi != 0). The wrong sign follows from psi < 0: Cov(i(-1), error) = b psi var(nu)/(1 - psi c).
