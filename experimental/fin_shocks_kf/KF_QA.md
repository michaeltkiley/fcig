**KF-Q4 (open). The form of K1, the first relaxation.** Proposal: **latent daily macro that nests Note B exactly.**
- Within month t, the daily latent macro moves with the day's financial move: m_tau = m_{tau-1} + Gamma (f_tau - f_{tau-1}) + nu_tau (nu small daily noise, variance estimated).
- On the first day of each month the level is set by Note B's monthly equation, with the observed monthly averages as lags: m = c_m + sum A_l mbar_{t-l} + sum G_l fbar_{t-l} + e_t.
- Observation: the monthly data are the monthly averages of the daily latent series (u a stock averaged over the month; pi the average of the daily annualised rate), so they fit Note B's definitions.
- The daily block conditions on yesterday's latent macro m_{tau-1} (what has happened so far) in place of the month's final value m_t (relaxes M3), with the previous month's average as Note B's second macro term.
- With Gamma = 0 and nu = 0 the latent series is constant within the month, and the model is exactly Note B (M1, M2, M3 restored). So the relaxation is one set of parameters (Gamma, var nu) with a known null.
- What identifies Gamma: an announcement early in the month moves more of that month's average than one late in the month, so the monthly macro surprises co-move with the within-month timing of financial moves. Identifying assumption, stated plainly: within a day, finance moves first (same-day macro news that moves finance is attributed to Gamma).
- Size: latent state about 2(L+1) + 2 elements; the filter is fast. Inference cannot use Note B's bootstrap of daily rows (resampled rows are not a time series), so it needs a choice (KF-Q5, after this one): I would propose a Gibbs sampler with a simulation smoother and flat priors on the Note B coefficients, reporting 90 percent posterior intervals.
Alternative: K1 without the Gamma channel (only M3 relaxed: daily block conditions on the filtered latent macro). Smaller, but leaves the macro-first restriction in place, which is the one the state space is best placed to test.

**KF-R3 (14 Nov 2026). [result, r*-gap with the FCI, 12 lags]** K1 estimated by ECME (`03_k1_fit.py`; stopped at the 500-cycle cap, not converged: gain 0.00075 per cycle at the end; the likelihood is a long flat ridge). Null check by simulation (`04_k1_sim.py`, `output/k1_sim.csv`): 16 samples of Note B's estimated model (true Gamma = 0) on the actual calendar, resampled residuals, K1 estimated the same way (300-cycle cap; 9 of 16 hit it).
- **Joint test of the K1 channel (Gamma, S_nu) against Note B:** LR 19.2 on the data at the same 300-cycle cap (19.6 at 500). Null distribution: median 6.8, 75th percentile 8.7, 90th 16.9; one of 16 null samples exceeds 19.2 (20.9). Monte Carlo p-value (1 + 1) / (16 + 1) = 0.12: **not significant at the 10 percent level**. The chi-square(7) reference (90th percentile 12.0) would wrongly reject; the likelihood-ratio statistic does not have its textbook distribution here (variance parameters on the boundary under the null). With 16 draws the p-value is imprecise.
- **Unemployment channel:** same-day move 0.43 pp per +1 pp on the 2-year on the data; in the null samples mean 0.16, standard deviation 0.30 (range -0.30 to 0.88). Inside the null range: not distinguishable from zero.
- **Inflation channel:** Gamma_pi = -1.47 (2-year) and -1.66 (FCI) on the data; in the null samples the most negative values are -0.56 and -1.02 (standard deviations 0.46 and 0.53). **Outside the range of all 16 null samples**; same-day inflation move -3.1 (annualised monthly rate) per +1 pp on the 2-year. Not a pre-specified test [inference: the component lies outside the null range, but the joint test does not reject; a component test needs more null draws].
- **Responses** (point, per +1 pp on the 2-year in the window): unemployment 0.45 / 0.53 / 0.37 at 12 / 24 / 36 months (Note B 0.34 / 0.50 / 0.43); price level -0.68 / -1.16 / -1.44 (Note B -0.42 / -0.81 / -1.11). Unemployment moves earlier (0.19 in the event month, 0.37 at 3 months against 0.00 and 0.22) and ends where Note B does; the price level falls about 0.3 pp more, from the inflation channel. No intervals yet (KF-Q5).

**KF-A3 (14 Nov 2026, user). [decided] K1 closed.** The author is comfortable with Note B's a priori restriction of no macro response within the month. K1 shows that a within-month response cannot be identified in this structure: the likelihood is flat along the Gamma direction, and the joint test does not reject against a simulated null (KF-R3). If the paper adopts the Kalman-filter approach, this is a footnote: 'We explored whether a within-month response of unemployment and inflation to financial conditions is identified; given the structure, the likelihood is flat in that direction, so we retain the restriction.' The inflation-channel lead is not pursued.
