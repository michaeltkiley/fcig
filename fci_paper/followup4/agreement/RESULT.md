# Do the market and FRB/US weights agree, and does the disagreement matter?

**Question.** The two weightings agree on a conventional policy surprise. How far does that carry to asset-price combinations that occur in the economy; is the gap about asset classes or assumed persistence; does it survive a like-for-like comparison? Code: `replication/18_agreement.py` [verified: 16 s, deterministic].

**Design.** psi: responses to one unit of NS news (10-year +0.53 pp, equity valuation -7.8, dollar +4.2); w_M market GLS weights; b_h FRB/US GDP effect at h quarters of a persistent shift, over that of a persistent 1 pp funds-rate rise (h = 2, 4 main, 8), fixed; w_B,h = b_h/(b_h'psi). psi and w_M bootstrapped (500 announcement draws); intervals are 5th-95th percentiles. Asset paths are priced from FRB/US temporary-shock responses; persistent equals cumulated temporary within 0.0003 at h = 4 [verified].

**Numbers.**
- b_h'psi (market = 1): 0.925 [0.83, 1.03] (h=2), 0.975 [0.87, 1.09] (h=4), 0.930 [0.83, 1.04] (h=8). w_B,4 = (0.584, -0.032, 0.103); w_M = (0.609, -0.051, 0.065).
- Example (h=4): equity -5: 0.256 market, 0.158 FRB/US; dollar +3.9: 0.255, 0.392. FRB/US dollar/equity ratio 2.15, 2.48, 2.53 at h = 2, 4, 8; market 0.995 [0.34, 2.30]; ratio of ratios at h=4 2.49 [1.02, 6.8] (h=2: 2.16 [0.89, 5.9]).
- Matched paths (h=4): half-lives 8, 4, 2 quarters give ratios 2.50, 2.52, 2.55 (persistent 2.48); h=2: 2.16-2.19. b'psi falls to 0.90, 0.83 [0.74, 0.93], 0.73. Unequal persistence by asset moves the ratio only from 1.9 to 3.4; no cell reaches 1.
- Persistence data [inference]: post-1996 AR(1) half-lives of levels 20, 21, 26 quarters; daily equity response to NS news does not decay within 20 days (1.9 x day 0), uninformative later; no daily 10-year or dollar data.
- D_4 on held-out announcements: mean |D| 0.010 against a market move of 0.053 (ratio 0.19 [0.09, 0.36]); |D| above half the move in 25 percent [13, 45]; never above 0.25.
- D_4 on ordinary movements, nominal, 1996-2026: monthly mean |D| 0.049 [0.03, 0.13] (ratio to market move 0.25), above 0.25 in 0.5 percent of months; 12-month changes: mean |D| 0.24 [0.12, 0.59] (ratio 0.33 [0.18, 0.69]), above 0.25 in 39 percent of months [12, 74], above 0.5 in 12 percent [0, 50]; runs above 0.25 average 7.9 months [3.5, 10.2], longest 29. r* step and 1973-2026 are similar.
- Bridge F - M (r*, levels): corr 0.94 [0.83, 0.98]. Share of variance: equity weights 0.72 [-0.01, 0.91], dollar weights 0.14, housing 0.08, spreads 0.04, policy and normalization about 0; real and r* transformations about 0 (-0.07 together). 12-month changes: equity weights 0.49, dollar 0.18, mortgage spread 0.17, housing 0.09 [0.00, 0.24], BBB 0.04; the r* equity transformation alone 0.20.
- Episodes (r*, F - M): 1998-2001 +1.73 [0.27, 3.03], equity weights 1.29 (78 percent), dollar 0.23. 2004-06 -0.17 [-0.81, 0.41]: equity weights +0.65 offset by housing -0.73. 2022-24 +0.96 [-0.05, 1.79]: equity 0.53, dollar 0.52, mortgage spread 0.31, housing -0.39; the r* step cuts the equity term from 0.84 to 0.53.

**Verdict.** The 2.4 contrast is not a persistence artefact: it holds at every common half-life, and its size is not credibly eliminated by unequal persistence. It is imprecise on the market side (interval of the ratio of ratios barely excludes 1 at h = 4, 8, not at h = 2). Raw agreement (0.97) holds for persistent paths only; with decaying paths it falls (0.83 at half-life 4), so compare normalized weights. The difference D is small on announcements (a fifth of the move) and about a third of the market movement over 12 months, exceeding 0.25 points in two-fifths of months, in 8-month runs: economically sizeable for years-long swings, not for monthly noise. The "equities" attribution holds for 1998-2001 and for the level variance, but not for 2004-06 (housing) or 2022-24 (dollar and spreads equal it).

**For the paper.** Lead with b_h'psi and w_B,h with intervals; present D_h as the quantity that decides how often the choice matters; state the dollar-equity contrast as a GDP-effect difference robust to persistence; attribute episodes by stage, not to equities alone. Caveats: b_h fixed (intervals too narrow); block joints break runs; no h > 8.
