# Announcement against non-announcement movements at the same horizon

**Question.** Is the disagreement D = (w_B,4 - w_M)'x larger outside announcements than in them once the horizon is held fixed? Code: `replication/20_daily.py` (helper `lib/daily_core.py`), data `replication/data/daily/` [verified: 4 s, deterministic].

**Design.** Daily 10-year yield (DGS10), nominal broad dollar (DTWEXB before 2006, DTWEXBGS after), 100 ln daily CAPE; 1996-01 to 2026-09, 7,636 days, 251 announcement days (scheduled USMPD meetings, same exclusions as `events_raw`; March-April 2020 and dropped events excluded). Weights as step 18 (b_4'psi = 0.975), bootstrap of the weights (500 draws) joint with an iid resample of announcement windows and a block resample of non-announcement windows. Part A: windows of 1, 5, 22 days ending on announcements against non-overlapping announcement-free windows. Part B: ladder of 1, 5, 22, 66, 252 days on identical end dates, total and with announcement days zeroed ('exann'). Three primary comparisons (part A) and three contrasts (part B); intervals are 5th-95th percentiles.

**Numbers (ratio = mean |D| / mean |market move|).**
- Daily: announcement days 0.29 [0.16, 0.56] (n = 251), other days 0.31 [0.17, 0.62] (n = 7,339); difference -0.021 [-0.096, 0.006]; ratio of ratios 0.93 [0.80, 1.03].
- Weekly (5 days): announcement 0.26 [0.14, 0.56] (251), other 0.28 [0.15, 0.59] (1,265); difference -0.020 [-0.075, 0.028].
- 22 days: announcement 0.26 [0.15, 0.56] (248), other 0.25 [0.13, 0.52] (98); difference +0.016 [-0.047, 0.117].
- Share with |D| above half the market move: announcement 0.28, 0.32, 0.33 and other 0.33, 0.31, 0.29 at 1, 5, 22 days; differences' intervals all include zero.
- Ladder, non-announcement part ('exann'), same assets and end dates: 1 day 0.32 [0.18, 0.62], 5 days 0.29, 22 days 0.28 [0.16, 0.60], 66 days 0.31, 252 days 0.34 [0.19, 0.72]. Contrasts: 22 vs 1 -0.042 [-0.069, 0.004]; 252 vs 1 +0.023 [-0.066, 0.187]; 252 vs 22 +0.066 [-0.012, 0.198] (share above half the move: 0.32 at 1 day, 0.40 at 252 days; contrast +0.078 [-0.026, 0.162]). 'Total' ladder: 0.32, 0.28, 0.26, 0.30, 0.33.
- The step 18 values (0.19 intraday held out; 0.24 monthly; 0.33 12-month) against daily-data ratios (0.29 announcement day; 0.26 at 22 days; 0.33 at 252 days): the 12-month figure is reproduced on different data [verified]; the intraday 0.19 is lower than the daily announcement-day 0.29.

**Verdict.** At a fixed horizon the ratio is the same on announcement and other days: differences at 1, 5 and 22 days are -0.02, -0.02 and +0.02, intervals include zero [verified]. Across horizons on the same assets and dates it is flat from 0.32 at one day to 0.34 at 12 months; the rise is 0.02 [-0.07, 0.19], and the 22-day to 252-day rise 0.07 [-0.01, 0.20] is positive in 90 percent of draws [verified]. The ratio therefore does not depend on whether the move is an announcement; it also does not clearly rise with horizon on these assets. The earlier horizon gap (0.19 to 0.33) is mostly the difference between intraday windows, in which the policy surprise dominates and D vanishes by construction, and daily or longer data in which other news enters [inference]; it is not evidence that the asset mix outside announcements causes larger disagreement. Level of the ratio, about 0.3, is the same across all cells.

**Caveats.** Nominal dollar stands in for the real dollar and no r* adjustment (real adjustments move little over days to weeks) [inference]; weights in-sample on announcement days, b_4 fixed, so intervals are mostly the weight uncertainty and wide; 22-day non-announcement windows are few (98); a 12-month window always contains announcements, so 'exann' removes announcement days rather than finding announcement-free years.
