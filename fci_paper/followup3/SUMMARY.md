# Round 5 results: 1996+ and pseudo real time (11 Nov 2026)

Design agreed by both advisors (`../reviews/ADVISOR_*.md`, Round 5): weights fixed on 1996-2026 (they define the unit); expanding-window weight path from about 2006 as a drift check (20-year rolling descriptive only); VAR 1973+ main with a 1996+ check; regressions on a fixed sample with recursive forecast evaluation; no rolling estimation windows. Three analyses, run independently (I have not re-run them; numbers are as reported).

## Answer to the author's questions
- **How to do 1996+:** fixed 1996-2026 weights; expanding (recursive) forecast evaluation; no rolling-window estimation (about two recessions per 20-year window; the rolling weights fail only because early announcements drop out); the 1973+ VAR stays the main yardstick with the 1996+ line beside it.
- **Pseudo real time:** was not addressed before; now built where possible (vintages for unemployment, payrolls, NROU from 2011, LW r* from 2005). The honest claim is pseudo real time in the macro variables, not in the weights or r*; "out of sample" is not clean for the weights.
- **Does the foundation hold on 1996+?** Yes for the yardstick (about 0.6, wider interval) and the unemployment flow/stance results, at roughly one third to one half of the 1973+ size in R2 gain; no for payrolls; real-time r* weakens the flow result. Note: the earlier subperiod estimates (0.65, 0.39, -0.01 for 1976-89, 1990-2007, 2009-19) and the pooled 1996+ estimate (0.73) are not reconciled; pooled 1996+ includes 2008 and the 2021-26 period.
